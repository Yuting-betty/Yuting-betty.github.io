My research adopts a quantitative and computational approach to urban and regional questions. I combine **spatial econometrics, network analysis, large-scale spatial data, bibliometric analysis, and computational methods** to investigate complex spatial and relational processes.

I am particularly interested in developing and applying methods that connect the territorial and network dimensions of urban and regional development.

### Methods and approaches

- Spatial econometrics
- Social and intercity network analysis
- Large-scale spatial data analysis
- Bibliometric and scientometric analysis
- GIS and spatial analysis
- Computational and machine-learning methods
