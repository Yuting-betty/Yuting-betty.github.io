A second strand of my research focuses on **intercity networks and relational urban development**. Rather than treating cities as isolated territorial units, I examine how flows and relationships between cities shape their economic and innovative development.

My work investigates **intercity cooperation and competition, innovation networks, knowledge collaboration, and knowledge circulation**. I am particularly interested in how cities occupy different positions within regional and global networks and how these relational structures interact with territorial organization.

### Selected research themes

- Intercity cooperation and competition
- Innovation and knowledge networks
- Knowledge collaboration and circulation
- Network externalities
- Multi-locational firms and intercity relations
- Territorial embeddedness and cross-boundary connections
